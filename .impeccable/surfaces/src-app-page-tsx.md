# Surface brief: IITIConnect (home + whole app), soft redesign

Scope: home (Persuade) and every signed-in screen, sign-in and privacy (Operate/Read in the same world). Audience: IIT Indore students. Action: sign in with IIT Indore Google; post a trip (destination, time, seats, open to bring items) or ask a traveller to bring something. Avoid: corporate template and the previous fest-poster look (user rejected it). Keep: test-visible labels, the independent-project disclaimer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm spring-morning travel app: content floats on white rounded cards over a blush-to-mint sky, like a modern travel booking site. Refuses the loud poster and the grey SaaS dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Sky ground #fbf7f4 with blush (#fde2e4), peach (#ffe8d6) and mint (#dff3e4) radial gradients; drawn SVG hills and a winding dashed road that fade into the page on public pages. White cards, 24-28px radius, soft offset shadows. Ink #1c2333, muted #5b6474. One coral action colour #cf4416 for primary pills; green only for confirmed. Headlines split a light Bodoni Moda serif phrase with bold Manrope; body Manrope.

STORY: Visitor sees a friendly headline, a labelled sample of trips leaving campus, and one coral Google button. Inside, trips are cards; posting is one short card; asking to bring an item lives on the trip page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: "Going to the city?" in serif over "Go together." in bold sans, one line of copy, coral Google button. Right (below on phones): floating white card "Leaving campus soon" with four sample rows and seat dots. Hills and road fill the bottom of the hero.

FORM: Pinned by the user's reference screenshot (soft travel landing with floating search card); no roll. Signature motion: cards rise in once on load; hover lifts on trip cards.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
